FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything You Need to Know

How does Pluto AI guarantee zero raw PII leaks? +

Pluto AI implements an on-device Privacy Shield before any data leaves your browser. It uses Luhn checksum verification for credit cards, contextual DOM attribute markers for password fields, and strict regular expressions for emails, phones, and IDs. Sensitive data is replaced with hashed tokens (e.g., [REDACTED_CARD_VISA_8812]), and the payload is encrypted using AES-GCM-256 before inference.

Which AI models and providers are supported? +

Pluto AI supports Google Gemini (Gemini 2.5 Flash Lite with 500 free requests per day, Gemini 2.5 Pro), OpenAI (GPT-4o, GPT-4o-mini), Anthropic (Claude 3.5 Sonnet), Groq, DeepSeek, Together AI, xAI Grok, Cohere, custom endpoints, and a 100% Offline Simulator mode.

Which browsers are compatible with Pluto AI? +

Pluto AI is built on standard Chrome Manifest V3 Side Panel APIs and works seamlessly across Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Brave Browser, Arc Browser, Opera, and Vivaldi.

Do I need to pay or provide a credit card? +

No! Pluto AI is completely free to download and use. You can bring your own free API keys (like Google AI Studio free tier) or use the built-in offline test engine without spending a dime.
